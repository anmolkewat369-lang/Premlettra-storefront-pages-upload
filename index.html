A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Everything clear before you send the butterflies.

When do I get my website link?+

During our early-access launch, we'll confirm your order and delivery time over WhatsApp. Automatic checkout and instant publishing are planned for the next version.

How long will the link work?+

Every plan includes 30 days online, starting from the day your link is delivered. We'll show the exact expiry date with your order.

Can anyone see my website?+

Your page is made to be shared with a personal link. Anyone who has that link may be able to open it, so please avoid uploading anything you don't want to share.

Can I use my own song or photos?+

You can add your own photos when you have permission to use them. Music options depend on the selected plan; use only tracks you have rights to share.

What if I need help with my order?+

Message us on WhatsApp and include your package choice. We'll confirm the next step with you.
